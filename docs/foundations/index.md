# Chapter 1: Foundations of UX design

Welcome to the first chapter. It introduces the ideas that every later topic builds on: what user experience is, who works on it, how UX designers approach problems, and why research and empathy matter so much.

You don't need any previous experience to read this chapter. Each page explains new terms the first time they appear, and you can always look them up in the [glossary](./14-glossary).

## What you will learn

1. **What UX is** - what "user experience" means and which qualities make an experience good.
2. **Who works in UX** - the roles in and around UX design, and what an entry-level designer actually does.
3. **How products are made** - the product development life cycle, from the first idea to launch.
4. **How to put people first** - user-centered, universal, inclusive, and equity-focused design, plus accessibility.
5. **How people use technology differently** - assistive technology and designing for many platforms.
6. **How to solve design problems** - the design thinking framework and each of its five phases.
7. **How teams work fast** - design sprints and retrospectives.
8. **How to learn about users** - UX research methods and the biases that can distort them.
